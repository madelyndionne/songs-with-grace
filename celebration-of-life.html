Devotional

A Life Worth Celebrating

Celebration Of Life

Scripture: Matthew 5:16; Philippians 1:3; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18

Every life is a collection of moments. Some are remembered with laughter, others with tenderness. A word of encouragement, a willing hand, a shared prayer or a simple act of kindness can leave an impression that continues to shape the lives of others long after the moment has passed.

Paul writes in Philippians 1:3, “I thank my God upon every remembrance of you.” What a beautiful way to remember someone we have loved. Each precious memory becomes an opportunity to thank God for the privilege of having shared a portion of our earthly journey with them.

In Celebration Of Life, we are invited to recognize the blessings that came through another person's life. The closing words of the chorus direct our attention toward the greatest source of those blessings:

With grateful hearts, we celebrate
A life that reflected... God who is Great.

Jesus tells us in Matthew 5:16, “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.” What a wonderful legacy it is when remembering someone's life leads us to glorify God.

For those who are in Christ, there is also the precious promise of reunion. In 1 Thessalonians 4:17, we read, “and so shall we ever be with the Lord.” Our memories give us reasons for gratitude today, while His promise gives us reason to rejoice in what lies ahead.

Perhaps the most meaningful way to celebrate a life is to carry forward the kindness, encouragement and love we received, allowing God's light to shine through our own lives.

Reflection

Whose life has helped you recognize the goodness of God? How might you carry forward the blessings you received through that person?

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for the precious lives You have woven into our own. Thank You for the memories that bring smiles, the lessons that continue to guide us and the love that has helped shape who we are. Comfort those who grieve, fill our hearts with gratitude, and help us reflect Your goodness in the lives of others. Thank You for the promise of eternal life through Jesus Christ. Amen.
