Let's Talk About Your Song

Tell me a little about your occasion and when you need the song. A personal phone interview is included to discuss your story and musical preferences. We'll work out the details together.

Request a Custom Song — select Custom Song in the contact form. You may include a phone number and a convenient time to call; my phone number is not published.

What You'll Receive

I'll reply by email to acknowledge your request and arrange our conversation. Once we've confirmed the details, I'll email you two comparable, finished MP3 versions of the same song within two weeks. Both are yours to keep.

Privacy & Use

Your privacy comes first. Songs with personal names, events, or identifying stories remain private unless you expressly authorize publication. I'll discuss any permissions with you and confirm them by email; publication requires your written authorization. Songs with universal themes and no identifying private details may be published.

Copyright remains with the author, Madelyn Dionne. You may share your finished song for personal, noncommercial use. Commercial use or alterations require written permission.

After Delivery

I'd love to hear what you think. Your delivery email may invite you to submit a review for publication, only with your permission. If you'd like to leave a voluntary tip after receiving your song, you may use PayPal at MadelynDionneWrites@gmail.com. No tip is expected, and the amount is entirely your choice.
