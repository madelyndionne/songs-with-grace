About Songs With Grace

Ordinary Songs. Eternal Messages.

Songs With Grace grew from a simple desire: to use ordinary things and everyday experiences to point toward spiritual truth. Water, seeds, roads, waiting, memory, work, questions—even humor—can become places where faith and life meet.

Songs With Grace exists to welcome people into a simple, thoughtful place where original songs, devotionals, and Scripture work together to encourage reflection, spiritual growth, and deeper engagement with God’s Word. Everything is designed to be easy to use, available at the point of need, and free of unnecessary barriers—so visitors can simply listen, read, explore, and stay awhile.

About Madelyn

Madelyn Dionne is my pen name. My spiritual journey began when I received Jesus as a young child. Later, I spent five years as a church secretary, along with many years as a wife, mother, and grandmother. Through these roles, other jobs, countless hours spent crafting, and the experiences of ordinary life, the years have shaped both who I am and the ideas I bring to my writing.

I am now retired, writing from the heart and seeking to speak plainly about faith in ordinary life. I believe God has called me to this work.

I write the lyrics myself and use AI-assisted music tools to shape the recordings, choosing the voices, instruments, and arrangement until the song says what I mean.

Besides the songs gathered here, there are others housed on SoundCloud, where listeners first began finding and sharing my music.

Why the Site Works the Way It Does

The details here are intentional, but they are meant to stay quietly in the background. Favorites appear when you want to remember a song. A playlist is there when you want to keep listening. Devotionals offer a place to go deeper. Scripture is available at the point where you may want to read it for yourself.

No account is required to listen, read, save Favorites, or build a Playlist. The goal is not to make visitors learn a website. It is to make the website serve the visitor.

Scripture Comes First

The songs and devotionals may illustrate, reflect, or point toward biblical truth, but Scripture is the authority. The hope is that what you find here does not stop with a song or a thought, but leads you back to God’s Word for yourself.

It is my hope that Songs With Grace will be a resource for growth, comfort, inspiration, a deeper understanding of God, and greater engagement with Scripture. Come in. Listen. Read. Explore. Stay awhile. You are welcome here.
