Gifts With Grace

Thoughtfully Made. Freely Serving.

Coming Soon!

Anticipated opening: December 2, 2026

Gifts With Grace is an extension of Songs With Grace, offering thoughtfully handcrafted gifts to encourage, comfort and celebrate.

Our first offerings will include genuine freshwater pearl earrings, with a special opportunity to share a little encouragement with someone else. Personalized songs are also part of this growing ministry.

Our handcrafted gifts are priced to recover costs, with our time and labor freely given. Additional support for the ministry is always welcome and entirely voluntary.

In the meantime, we invite you to listen to Perfect Pearl, the song behind our pearl gifts.

We look forward to sharing more with you soon!
