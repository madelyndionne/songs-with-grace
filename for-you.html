Devotional

For You

“For of him, and through him, and to him, are all things: to whom be glory for ever. Amen.” — Romans 11:36 (KJV)

Every wonder we discover was known to God before we discovered it. Every principle we uncover, every mystery we begin to understand, every possibility we have yet to imagine lies within the fullness of His knowledge.

“Great is our Lord, and of great power: his understanding is infinite.” — Psalm 147:5 (KJV)

We stand within a creation whose vastness exceeds our comprehension, fashioned by the One whose greatness is unsearchable. The smallest detail bears witness to His wisdom. The heavens declare His glory. All things consist by Him.

And within the immeasurable greatness of His creation, He has given us a place.

“For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.” — Ephesians 2:10 (KJV)

What a profound privilege that the Creator of all things should entrust His workmanship with work of eternal significance. The mind that discovers, the hand that creates, the voice that sings — each draws upon what He has first given.

Even our capacity to marvel at Him is His gift.

We see through a glass, darkly. What we understand is but a glimpse. Yet there awaits a day when we shall see face to face and know even as we are known.

Until then, we stand in wonder before the One from whom all things come, through whom all things consist, and for whom all things were created.

“Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.” — Revelation 4:11 (KJV)

Prayer

To Him be glory forever.

Amen.
