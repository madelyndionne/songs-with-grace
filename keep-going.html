Devotional

The Race Already Set Before Us

There are mornings when continuing feels natural, and others when “tired is the word of the day.” Scripture does not pretend those days do not exist. Hebrews 12:1–2 describes the Christian life as a race that has been set before us and calls us to run it with patience while looking unto Jesus. The course is not invented one exhausted step at a time. We are called to continue faithfully on the course God has given us.

The song, Keep Going, does not promise that every answer will arrive today. It says something quieter: “No, the Answer doesn’t come today / But you Continue anyway.” Galatians 6:9 tells us not to grow weary in well doing. James 1:2–4 connects the testing of faith with patience and maturity. Perseverance is not evidence that nothing is happening. Sometimes perseverance is part of what God is using to grow us.

Roots Grow Where No One Sees

A tree can appear unchanged while an unseen root system is extending beneath the soil. Spiritual growth can be like that. Colossians 2:6–7 speaks of walking in Christ while being rooted and built up in Him. Philippians 1:6 gives confidence that the God who began a good work in His people will continue it.

That makes the chorus especially important: “Roots grow where no one sees.” We often judge growth by what is immediately visible—an answer, a change, a result. God is not restricted to visible progress. His Word can be deepening trust, endurance, humility, obedience, and dependence long before we can point to an outward result.

The One Who Goes Before

The bridge turns perseverance into something more than determination: “You have never walked behind me. / You have always gone before.” Deuteronomy 31:8 describes the Lord as the One who goes before His people and does not forsake them. Psalm 139:1–6 speaks of God's complete knowledge of us, and Psalm 37:23–24 describes the steps of a person whose way is upheld by the Lord.

This does not mean every road is easy or that every choice we make was prescribed by God. It means we never travel beyond His knowledge, presence, or ability to guide. The Christian's confidence is not, I know exactly where this road goes. It is, God knows where I am, He knows what lies ahead, and He has not abandoned me here.

One More Step

Sometimes keep going means a great act of courage. More often it means the next faithful step: pray again, obey what Scripture has already made clear, do today's work, receive today's grace, and leave tomorrow in God's hands. Isaiah 40:31 connects waiting upon the Lord with renewed strength. The strength is His provision; the continuing is our response.

The final chorus changes one word that reveals what the journey has been doing all along: “He's been growing you and me.” The road is not merely something to finish. God can use the walking itself to form us.

So when joy is present, keep going. When answers are absent, keep going. When growth is hidden beneath the surface, keep going. Not because persistence saves us, and not because sheer willpower can carry us indefinitely, but because Christ is worthy of our trust and God remains faithful in the next step.

Prayer

Father, when I am tired, impatient, or unable to see what You are doing, help me remain faithful in the step You have given me today. Keep me from measuring Your work only by what I can see. Root me more deeply in Christ, strengthen me through Your Word, and grow in me the endurance I need.

Thank You that You know the road before me and that I do not walk beyond Your presence. When answers do not come today, teach me to trust You without surrendering obedience. Give me grace for one more faithful step, and use even the unseen parts of this journey to make me more like Christ. In Jesus' name, Amen.
