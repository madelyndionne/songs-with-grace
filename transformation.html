Devotional

The Work Within

Scripture Focus: 2 Corinthians 3:18

Three jars sit quietly in a warm kitchen. Each holds something different, yet all are undergoing a similar process. What began as ordinary ingredients is slowly becoming something new. The changes are not immediately visible, but the process is underway. Given time, air, and proper care, the contents will develop the distinctive qualities of vinegar.

There is something familiar about that quiet process.

God's work in our lives often takes place gradually. We may recognize areas where we need to grow, but we cannot always see what He is accomplishing from one day to the next. Philippians 1:6 reminds us that He who began a good work in us will continue it until the day of Jesus Christ. His work has a beginning, a purpose, and a promised completion.

The One Who Changes Us

Second Corinthians 3:18 describes believers being changed into the image of Christ, from glory to glory, by the Spirit of the Lord. This is the transformation that matters most: becoming increasingly like the Savior who redeemed us.

The jars cannot determine what they will become through their own efforts. Likewise, spiritual transformation is not simply a matter of deciding to become a better person. It is the continuing work of God in those who belong to Christ.

Jesus teaches in John 15:4–5 that branches bear fruit by abiding in the vine. Our spiritual growth depends upon our relationship with Him. As we abide in Christ, His Word shapes our understanding, His Spirit works within us, and His grace teaches us to live according to His will.

The Fragrance That Reaches Beyond

As vinegar develops, its distinctive aroma may begin to fill the kitchen. What was taking place quietly within the jars becomes noticeable beyond them.

Paul uses the imagery of fragrance in 2 Corinthians 2:14–15, describing how God makes the knowledge of Christ known through His people. He calls believers the sweet savour of Christ unto God.

That is a beautiful purpose for the changes God makes in us.

As He develops patience, kindness, humility, faithfulness, and love in our lives, those qualities affect the people around us. A gentle response, a generous act, a word of encouragement, or a willingness to forgive may become an opportunity for someone else to recognize something of Christ.

The transformation within begins to bear fruit beyond ourselves.

Created for His Purpose

Ephesians 2:10 reminds us that we are God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God has prepared for us to walk in.

We are not transformed merely for our own satisfaction. God is preparing us to serve Him and to bless others through the work He has entrusted to us.

The three jars may eventually become three very different gifts, each useful in its own way. Likewise, God gives His children different abilities, opportunities, and places of service. Their usefulness comes not from being identical, but from being faithful to His purpose.

And so we return to the words of the final chorus of Transformation:

“Each in His time, each in His way / We become more like Him, every day.”

May the work God is accomplishing within us become increasingly evident in lives that honor Christ and share His goodness with others.

Prayer

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for loving me as I am, while patiently working to make me more like Your Son. Sometimes I struggle to see the changes You're making in me. Sometimes I grow impatient with myself, wanting to be further along than I am.

Help me trust Your timing and recognize Your hand in the quiet, ordinary moments of my life. Show me where I need to grow, where I need to let go, and where I need to yield more fully to Your Spirit.

Lord, let the work You're doing within me reach beyond me. May others experience Your kindness, grace, and love through the way I live and the way I treat them.

Keep my heart close to Yours, and continue shaping me into the person You created me to be.

In Jesus' name, Amen.
