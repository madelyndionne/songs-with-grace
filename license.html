Free Use License

These songs and devotionals are here to be heard, read, and shared freely.

What you may do

Madelyn Dionne grants permission, without a fee, to use the original lyrics, sound recordings, and devotionals on Songs With Grace for personal, church, ministry, and educational purposes that are not commercial. You may:

  • Listen, download, and share unaltered copies with others.
  • Print and distribute lyrics or devotionals without charge.
  • Perform the songs in worship services, church gatherings, or other free, noncommercial events.
  • Include the songs in free church livestreams or recordings of those gatherings.

When sharing a recording, lyrics, or devotional online or in print, please credit Madelyn Dionne and link to Songs With Grace when practical. No public announcement or on-screen credit is required during a worship service.

What requires permission

Please obtain written permission before selling the material, charging for access, including it in anything offered for sale, using it in advertising or promotion of a business, or making any other commercial use. Please also ask before changing, remixing, or releasing a new recording or adaptation of a song or devotional.
