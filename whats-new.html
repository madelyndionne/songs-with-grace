✨ What’s New at SWG

A quick look at what has recently been added, changed, or improved at Songs With Grace.

A changelog, for the techies among us.

October 22, 2026

New Song — Möbius

Möbius joins Songs With Grace with music, cover artwork, lyrics, and a Scripture-first devotional exploring how God’s Word illuminates understanding, reveals connections, and invites us to return to the Scriptures.

Website Improvements — Downloads, Printing & Signup

Song pages now offer one Free Use License agreement route for MP3 downloads. A printing note explains how to use extra devotional pages for notes or select only the pages needed. The timed email signup popup has been removed; the regular signup remains available.

October 14, 2026

New Song — Just Enough

Just Enough joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a devotional on surviving devastation and finding strength in weakness. It is paired with Never Alone, offering a quiet progression from endurance to reassurance.

New Song — Never Alone

Never Alone joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a Scripture-centered devotional about God's presence, comfort in grief, and the love of Christ that never forsakes His people.

October 13, 2026

New Song — Calibration of the Heart

Calibration of the Heart joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a Scripture-centered devotional about returning to God’s unchanging truth instead of shifting human standards.

New Song — Jagged Edges

Jagged Edges joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a Scripture-centered devotional about salvation by grace and the new life Christ gives.

New Song — Stranger Going Home

Stranger Going Home joins SWG with music, cover artwork, lyrics, and a Scripture-centered devotional about living gratefully and faithfully while looking forward to the home Christ has promised.

New Song — At His Feet

At His Feet joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a Scripture-centered devotional on faithful stewardship, seeking God’s direction, and entrusting the results of our work to Him.

New Song — You Are Loved

You Are Loved joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a gentle Scripture-centered devotional about God’s abiding love, His care in suffering, and the sure hope found in Jesus Christ.

October 12, 2026

New Song — Shimmer In The Mirror

Shimmer In The Mirror begins a two-song sequence with a Scripture-first devotional on conviction, repentance, salvation through Christ, and the Holy Spirit received at conversion.

New Song — After The Shimmer

After The Shimmer joins SWG as the second part of a two-song sequence. The devotional explores the Holy Spirit’s indwelling at conversion, His continuing work in the believer, and preparation for service and good works.

October 11, 2026

New Song — When Things Go Right

When Things Go Right explores the joy of being called by God, serving Him, and seeing the fruit of that service.

October 9, 2026

Revised Song — Perfect Pearl

Perfect Pearl replaces the earlier Perfect Pearls version, with revised music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a new devotional exploring encouragement, the Source of good gifts, and sharing the Gospel of Christ.

Downloads — Free Use Agreement

Visitors can now request a song download from the Songs playlist. Before downloading, they are shown the Free Use License and must open it, scroll to the end, and check a single agreement box. Download requests are tracked privately through Plausible to help us understand which songs listeners choose to keep.

October 8, 2026

Additional Songs

  • Candlelight — surrender, Christlikeness, and the Holy Spirit.
  • Unfinished Business — added to the song catalog.
  • Eternal Dawning — added to the song catalog.
  • Mushroom Life — added to the song catalog.

New Song — The Score

The Score joins SWG with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a devotional exploring how stewardship and generosity can change what we count as success.

New Song — The Motions

The Motions is now live, with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a devotional about finding renewed joy in serving God even amid familiar daily routines. The song reflects on Revelation 2:2–5, Romans 12:2, and Luke 10:38–42.

October 7, 2026

Scripture Reading — Right on the Song Page

Scripture references can now be opened and read directly from song pages without leaving Songs With Grace. The new site-wide Scripture reader uses each song’s existing Scripture Map, keeping God’s Word available at the point of need while visitors listen and reflect.

New Song

I Have Called You has been added to SWG, with music, lyrics, devotional, prayer, Scripture Map, and in-page Scripture reading.

October 6, 2026

Playlists & Continuous Play

Visitors can now build a personal playlist directly from the song cards, arrange songs in the order they want, and use Play All for continuous listening. Playlists are saved in the visitor’s own browser, so no account or login is required.

October 5, 2026

The Rumbling

Revival has been renamed The Rumbling, with newly revised lyrics, music, cover artwork, devotional, and expanded Scripture Map. The song explores the quiet signs of spiritual awakening—from sensing that God is moving to recognizing together what He is calling us to become.

Email Signup Improved

The email signup popup was refined with a clear close control and improved behavior across site pages.

October 4, 2026
