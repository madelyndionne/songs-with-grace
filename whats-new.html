✨ What’s New at SWG

A quick look at what has recently been added, changed, or improved at Songs With Grace.

A changelog, for the techies among us.

October 8, 2026

New Song — The Motions

The Motions is now live, with music, lyrics, cover artwork, and a devotional about finding renewed joy in serving God even amid familiar daily routines. The song reflects on Revelation 2:2–5, Romans 12:2, and Luke 10:38–42.

October 7, 2026

Scripture Reading — Right on the Song Page

Scripture references can now be opened and read directly from song pages without leaving Songs With Grace. The new site-wide Scripture reader uses each song’s existing Scripture Map, keeping God’s Word available at the point of need while visitors listen and reflect.

New Song

I Have Called You has been added to SWG, with music, lyrics, devotional, prayer, Scripture Map, and in-page Scripture reading.

October 6, 2026

Playlists & Continuous Play

Visitors can now build a personal playlist directly from the song cards, arrange songs in the order they want, and use Play All for continuous listening. Playlists are saved in the visitor’s own browser, so no account or login is required.

October 5, 2026

The Rumbling

Revival has been renamed The Rumbling, with newly revised lyrics, music, cover artwork, devotional, and expanded Scripture Map. The song explores the quiet signs of spiritual awakening—from sensing that God is moving to recognizing together what He is calling us to become.

October 4, 2026

Site Improvements

  • Added Favorites* so visitors can save songs they want to return to.
  • Added My Favorites* filtering on the Songs page.
  • Added compact Share, Copy Link, Email, and Facebook* options to song pages.
  • Added a little introductory context to As the Worm Turns.
  • Expanded As the Worm Turns topics to include Creation, Joy, Humor, Gardening, and Worms.

October 5, 2026

Email Signup Improved

The email signup popup was refined with a clear close control and improved behavior across site pages.

October 4, 2026

Email Updates Added

Visitors can now sign up for email updates about new songs, devotionals, and site features. The signup was designed to stay unobtrusive, including a 14-day dismissal period.

September 30, 2026

Scripture Maps Strengthened

Scripture presentation was expanded so the biblical passages connected with each devotional are easier to identify and explore, while keeping Scripture as the authority behind the reflections.

September 29, 2026

Growing Catalog

The working Songs With Grace catalog reached 31 song pages as the site continued its rapid early expansion.

September 26, 2026

Analytics Added

Privacy-friendly analytics were added to help understand how visitors use Songs With Grace and which resources are being used.

September 25, 2026

Songs With Grace Launches

Songs With Grace officially went live.
