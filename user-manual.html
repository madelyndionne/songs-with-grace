Devotional

User Manual

Some manuals are easy to follow. Others seem to contain everything except the answer to the problem you actually have. You can turn pages, study diagrams, reread instructions—and still wonder, What am I supposed to do now?

Life can feel remarkably similar.

But the song, User Manual, turns that familiar frustration around and asks a much more personal question:

If you were a manual, what would the pages be?

Whether we realize it or not, people read our lives. They see how we respond when things go wrong. They notice how we treat someone who can do nothing for us. They watch what happens to our faith when prayers seem unanswered, when plans change, when we are hurt, disappointed, frightened, or simply tired.

What are those pages teaching?

Perhaps some are dog-eared because they contain lessons learned through difficulty. Some may have corrections written across them because experience taught us that what we once believed was wrong. There may be pages we wish we could tear out altogether.

But there are also pages of grace.

There are pages where God changed us. Pages where forgiveness replaced resentment. Pages where we discovered patience because we had no choice but to wait. Pages where we learned to trust God without knowing what He was doing. And perhaps there are pages whose importance we do not recognize at all—yet someone else has quietly highlighted them because something in the way we lived helped them find their own way forward.

That gives us reason to consider our lives carefully, but it does not make our lives the authority.

We are not the Holy Manual.

God has already given us His Word. Scripture provides the truth by which our lives are to be measured and shaped. It teaches, corrects, directs, warns, encourages, and equips us. When we do not know what belongs on our next page, we do not have to invent the answer. We can return to what God has already given us. (Psalm 119:105; Proverbs 3:5–6; 2 Timothy 3:16–17; James 1:22–25)

And as we follow His instruction, something beautiful happens: the truth written in Scripture begins to become visible in an ordinary human life.

Not perfectly. Not without crossed-out lines, stained pages, and lessons we sometimes have to learn more than once.

But visibly.

Love becomes something another person can witness. Mercy becomes something another person can receive. Faithfulness becomes something another person can remember. Grace becomes something another person can recognize because they encountered it through us. (Matthew 5:14–16; John 13:34–35; Galatians 5:22–23)

We are writing those pages every day.

So perhaps the question in the song is worth carrying with us:

If you were a manual, what would the pages be?

And maybe an even deeper question follows:

If someone read your life today, would its pages point them toward the One whose instructions you are trying to follow?
